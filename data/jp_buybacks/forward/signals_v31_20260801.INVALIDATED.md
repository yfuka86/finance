# INVALIDATED preview

The v3.1 snapshot applied data-quality, liquidity, and unit-lot gates but did not age the latest
monthly report to the as-of date. Expired and stale programs could therefore remain candidates.
It was invalidated before PnL evaluation or trading. Version 3.2 additionally requires an
unexpired program, at least 14 calendar days to its end, and a report no more than 45 days old.
